**Category:** Puzzle &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Platform:** iOS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Price:** Free

Stackagram is a word puzzle game that challenges you to unscramble five rows of letters into real words — then discover the hidden secret word running through the grid.

### Features

**Daily Puzzle:** A new puzzle every day. Solve it as fast as you can to earn up to 3 stars. Build your streak and track your progress on the daily calendar.

**500+ Levels:** Work through chapters of puzzles, each with a unique secret word to find. Earn stars to unlock new levels.

  <h2>Duration Dots</h2>
</div>

**Category:** Education &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Platform:** iOS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Price:** Free

Duration Dots is a simple tap-the-dots app designed to help those with special learning needs.

Choose how many dots you want — from 3 to 10 — and they appear in a row across the screen. Tap the dots one at a time, in order from left to right. Each tap fills a dot with color. When you fill the last dot, you get a celebration with confetti or fireworks and a completion message.

### Features

- Customize the dot color with a full color picker
- Set your own completion message
- Choose your celebration: confetti, fireworks, or none
- Add your own completion sound by importing audio from a video
