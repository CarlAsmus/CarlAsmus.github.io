## Stackagram

**Category:** Puzzle &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Platform:** iOS &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Price:** Free

Stackagram is a word puzzle game that challenges you to unscramble five rows of letters into real words — then discover the hidden secret word running through the grid.

### Features

**Daily Puzzle**
A new puzzle every day. Solve it in fewer moves to earn up to 3 stars. Build your streak and track your progress on the daily calendar.

**250+ Levels**
Work through chapters of hand-crafted puzzles, each with a unique secret word to find. Earn stars to unlock new chapters.
