Duration Dots does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage and are never sent to any server.

## Data Collection

We do not collect any data. No analytics, no tracking, no personal information.

## Third-Party Services

Duration Dots does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools.

## Children's Privacy

Duration Dots does not collect data from any user, including children.
